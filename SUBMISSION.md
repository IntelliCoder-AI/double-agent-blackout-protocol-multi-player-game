# Handshake submission draft

DOUBLE AGENT: BLACKOUT PROTOCOL is a browser-native 3D espionage deduction game for two cooperative investigators or 3–8 players with a hidden double agent. Every operative holds only part of a signed evidence trail. Explore the locked-down AEGIS headquarters, recover private records, authenticate them at physical terminals, and reconstruct a shared Living Evidence Wall before the final indictment.

A traitor can forge an export, jam verification, stall repairs or attempt an announced exfiltration. Every forgery preserves an immutable source, giving careful investigators a discoverable contradiction. Five handcrafted dossiers explore camera drift, reflected badge identifiers, time zones, sequence rollback and a midnight rollover. Guest identities and six-character room codes require no account registration; the backend validates roles, evidence, movement, sabotage, ballots, timers and scores.
